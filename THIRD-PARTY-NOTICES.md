## Fonts

The application can render its interface with the Noto Sans font family. Both
font files below are redistributed **unmodified**, in their original variable
font form, keeping their original family names. They are used together as a
fallback list: `Noto Sans` supplies Latin, Greek and Cyrillic glyphs, and
`Noto Sans SC` supplies the CJK glyphs that `Noto Sans` itself does not contain.

3. any modified version is distributed under the OFL and does not use the
